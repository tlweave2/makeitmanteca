The FEZ

Be Part of Something BIG. The Family Entertainment Zone (FEZ) is Manteca's premier destination for sports, recreation, and family fun. This 1,040-acre master-planned district is anchored by the Great Wolf Lodge and features Big League Dreams sports complex, Great Wolf indoor water park resort, and future retail, dining, and entertainment venues. Services

Tailored Solutions

Manteca's Office of Economic Development provides professional, free customized services to businesses, brokers, and developers to assist with market analysis, site selection, hiring, expedited permits and approvals, and tailored incentive solutions. We offer a dedicated, single point-of-contact for your project, ensuring clear, predictable city processes and guaranteed timelines designed to get you up and running on-time, and on-budget.

Site Selection

A thriving business starts with a great location, and with our in depth knowledge of Manteca's real estate market, we can help you find just the right site for your needs. We provide customized market analysis and personalized site search assistance. And we have a network of trusted developers and brokers to connect you with.

Talent

In today's market, access to talent is the most important factor in corporate location decisions and you need a reliable talent pipeline. That is why Manteca works closely with business prospects to address workforce needs early on. We provide customized labor market reports, personalized hiring plans, and connect you with our trusted workforce and university partners to provide qualified candidates, degree and certificate programs, and hiring incentives.

Fast-Tracked Approvals

The old adage is, time is money. We get that, which is why Manteca offers efficient approvals and innovative expediting programs. With our Project Review Team program, for key projects, we form a task force of senior staff who expedite your planning application, clearing a path for a rapid decision so you can get on with pulling that building permit. We pride ourselves on a business-friendly development services process designed to get you open sooner.

Small Business Support

All business is local, and small business is the lifeblood of any local economy, which is why Manteca has dedicated staff just to help small businesses. Our Small Business Support Specialists understand the unique needs and challenges you face and will assist you with any issue, big or small. We provide free business planning and financial consulting right at City Hall.

Startups

Economies that don't innovate stagnate. Which is why Manteca supports the launch, growth, and attraction of startup companies in Manteca, and the people and places that back them. Through our Startup Manteca Incentive Program, we are here to help your startup or incubator with targeted financial investment in your next great venture.

 

Incentives

Through our Economic Development Incentive Program, Manteca offers targeted financial incentives for companies or projects that make significant contributions to the City's employment and tax bases. In most cases, to qualify the project must create new high-quality jobs, generate significant new tax revenue, and fall within one of several target industry sectors. Incentive amounts are negotiable and can be tailored to meet the operating needs of your business. We also provide advisory services to help you apply for State of California incentives. All programs offer financing, site selection assistance, and expedited permits and approvals. Connect with us to find out what program is right for you.

Startups and Incubators

The Startup Manteca Incentive Program provides technical and financial assistance to launching and scaling startups, and for co-working and incubator facilities that support them. To qualify you must operate in Manteca and be an emerging or scaling company commercializing innovative products and platforms in most tech verticals.

View the Guidelines  Inquire About Eligibility

Breweries, Restaurants, and Wineries

The Brewery, Restaurant, and Winery Incentive Program, or BReW Program, provides financial support to target industry businesses in Manteca to cover expenses and costs associated with the development, improvement, or expansion of breweries, restaurants, and wineries.

View the Guidelines  Inquire About Eligibility

State and Federal Office

The State and Federal Office Incentive Program provides technical and financial assistance to state and federal government agencies adding or expanding office facilities in Manteca, with an emphasis on agency headquarters projects.

Learn more about State Incentives and Federal Incentives

Retail and Hospitality

For businesses and projects that generate significant new sales and occupancy taxes, and raise the bar on both lifestyle amenity quality and quantity, Manteca offers targeted tax rebate incentives.

Development Impact Fee Deferral

The Development Impact Fee Deferral Program allows a developer to defer payment of City assessed development impact fees that would otherwise be payable in full when a building permit is issued. This innovative best-in-class program functions as a low-interest source of project financing by allowing a developer to defer payment of fees for up to five years, at very low interest rates, with no payments for the first 36 months.

View the Guidelines  Inquire About Eligibility

Small Business Permit Assistance

The Permit Assistance Smart Start Program, or PASS, provides grants to small businesses to defray the cost of obtaining City planning and/or building permits and approvals required to construct or retrofit commercial space that the business will occupy. Small businesses are defined under the Program as businesses with less than 20 employees and revenues under $3 million dollars.

View the Guidelines  Access Application

Façade Improvement Program

The Façade Improvement through Exceptional Design program (FIXD) provides grant funding and professional design services for commercial building owners making exterior improvements to the appearance and/or functionality of their building façade. Improvements might include paint, lighting, awnings, signage, and similar enhancements.

View the Guidelines  Inquire About Eligibility
