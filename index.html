The Family Entertainment Zone

A 150+ acre master-planned destination district at the crossroads of Highway 99 and Highway 120 Bypass — the most significant shovel-ready development opportunity in California's Central Valley.

FEZ
Family Entertainment Zone
Manteca, California

The FEZ is designed as the premier entertainment and lifestyle hub of the Northern San Joaquin Valley. With world-class sports complexes, destination dining, retail, hospitality, and family attractions, this is where Northern California comes to play.

Strategically positioned with immediate freeway visibility to 150,000+ vehicles per day and surrounded by over 11,000 new homes in development, The FEZ offers unmatched market access and development potential.

Competitive Advantages

Why Manteca

Strategically located at the crossroads of California's major trade corridors, Manteca offers businesses unmatched access, a skilled workforce, and a city government that moves at the speed of business.

📈

Growing

One of California's fastest-growing cities. Population of 94K+ projected to reach 120K within the decade.

🔗

Connected

Highway 99, I-5, Highway 120 Bypass. Four international airports within 75 miles. BNSF/UP rail access.

⚡

Fast

Streamlined permitting & approvals. Concierge-level development services with dedicated project managers.

👥

Talented

830K+ regional workforce. Partnerships with San Joaquin Delta College, Stanislaus State, and UC Merced.

💲

Affordable

Significant savings on real estate, labor, and utilities versus Bay Area and Sacramento markets.

🌳

Active

Big League Dreams sports complex, 50+ parks, championship golf, Sierra Nevada & Delta nearby.

💰

Prosperous

Median household income above national average. Strong homeownership and growing consumer base.

🌎

Diverse

Vibrant multi-cultural community creating a dynamic workforce and consumer market.

📍

Close

75 miles to Silicon Valley, 60 miles to Bay Area, 45 miles to Stockton Port deep-water access.

🤝

Ready

A business-friendly partner. City leadership that actively invests in your success.
