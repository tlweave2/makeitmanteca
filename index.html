Why Manteca

At the crossroads of State Route 120, Interstate 5, and Highway 99 — in the heart of California's Central Valley — Manteca connects you to the Bay Area, Sacramento, and 30 million consumers. Four international airports sit within 70 miles, along with the Ports of Stockton and Oakland, ACE train service to the Bay Area, and direct freeway access.

Tailored Solutions

Site Selection

In-depth knowledge of Manteca's real estate market, customized market analysis, and a trusted network of developers and brokers.

Talent

Customized labor market reports, personalized hiring plans, and connections to workforce and university partners for qualified candidates.

Fast-Tracked Approvals

Small Business Support

Dedicated Small Business Support Specialists, plus free business planning and financial consulting right at City Hall.

Startups

Incentives

These programs are informational — an inquiry is not an application. Participation is highly selective and by invitation. Connect with our team to find out what's right for your project.

Startup Manteca Incentive Program

Technical and financial assistance for launching and scaling startups, and for co-working or incubator facilities that support them, across most tech verticals — AgTech, AI/ML, BioTech, Blockchain/Web3, CleanTech, and more.

Inquire View Guidelines (PDF)

BReW Program

Financial support for target industry businesses covering the development, improvement, or expansion of breweries, restaurants, and wineries in Manteca.

Inquire View Guidelines (PDF)

FIXD Program

The Façade Improvement through Exceptional Design program provides grant funding and professional design services for exterior building improvements — paint, lighting, awnings, signage, and more. Highly selective, invitation-only.

Inquire View Guidelines (PDF)

State & Federal Office Incentive Program

Technical and financial assistance for state and federal government agencies adding or expanding office facilities in Manteca, with an emphasis on agency headquarters projects.
