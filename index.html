The FEZ

The Family Entertainment Zone (FEZ) is a 150+ acre master-planned destination district at the heart of Manteca. Designed as the premier entertainment and lifestyle hub of the Northern San Joaquin Valley, The FEZ will feature world-class sports complexes, dining, retail, hospitality, and family attractions — all strategically positioned at the crossroads of Highway 99 and the Highway 120 Bypass.

With over 11,000 new homes approved and in development, a growing population of 94,000+, and immediate freeway visibility to 150,000+ vehicles per day, The FEZ represents one of the most significant shovel-ready development opportunities in California's Central Valley.

Why Manteca

Strategically located at the crossroads of California's major trade corridors, Manteca offers businesses unmatched access, a skilled workforce, and a city government that moves at the speed of business.

Business

Growing.
One of California's Fastest-Growing Cities

Population of 94,000+ with over 11,000 new homes approved. The Northern San Joaquin Valley's economic engine, projected to reach 120,000 residents within the decade.

Connected.
At the Crossroads of California

Immediate access to Highway 99, I-5, and Highway 120 Bypass. 150,000+ daily vehicles. Four international airports within 75 miles and direct BNSF/UP rail access.

Fast.
Streamlined Permitting & Approvals

Concierge-level development services with fast-tracked plan review, dedicated project managers, and a City Council committed to eliminating red tape.

Talented.
830,000+ Regional Workforce

Access to a deep labor pool across the Northern San Joaquin Valley. Partnerships with San Joaquin Delta College, Stanislaus State, and UC Merced for workforce training pipelines.

Affordable.
Lower Operating Costs

Significant savings on real estate, labor, and utilities compared to Bay Area and Sacramento markets. Competitive incentive programs to reduce startup and expansion costs.

Lifestyle

Active.
Year-Round Recreation

Big League Dreams sports complex, 50+ parks, 18-hole championship golf course, and the nearby Sierra Nevada mountains and Delta waterways for outdoor adventure.

Prosperous.
A Thriving Consumer Base

Median household income above the national average with strong homeownership rates. A growing middle class driving retail, dining, and entertainment demand.

Diverse.
Rich Cultural Fabric

A vibrant, multi-cultural community that brings together families from across California and beyond — creating a dynamic workforce and consumer market.

Close.
Minutes from Everywhere

75 miles to Silicon Valley, 60 miles to the Bay Area, 45 miles to Stockton Port (deep-water access), and 12 miles to Modesto. Central to everything that matters.

Ready.
A Business-Friendly Partner

A City Council and Economic Development team that actively invests in your success. Enterprise Zone benefits, responsive government, and a community that welcomes growth.

Our Services

Tailored Solutions

Free, customized business assistance from our Economic Development team — from initial concept to grand opening, we provide white-glove service at no cost to you.

Site Selection

Comprehensive market analysis, available property databases, demographic profiles, traffic studies, and guided site tours to help you find the perfect location.

Workforce & Talent

Customized labor market reports, hiring plan assistance, connections to local workforce development boards, and partnerships with regional colleges and trade schools.

Streamlined plan review, dedicated permit concierge, and a City Hall culture that prioritizes your timeline. We measure our plan review turnaround in days, not months.

Free business planning resources, permit assistance programs, façade improvement grants, and one-on-one counseling to help small businesses thrive in Manteca.
