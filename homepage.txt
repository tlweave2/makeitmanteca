### Why Elk Grove
#### Business (5 infographics)
1. Ready — "You won't find a more willing and sophisticated government partner"
2. Talented — 62% college educated, 70 academies, 15 industry sectors
3. Fast — First round plan review averages 7 days
4. Close — 75 miles to Silicon Valley, 12 miles to downtown Sacramento
5. Affordable — Up to 50% operating cost savings

#### Lifestyle (5 infographics)
1. Growing — Fastest growing mid-size economy in California, population 178K
2. Prosperous — 2nd best city for homeownership in CA, avg income $85K, avg home $450K
3. Diverse — 7th most multi-cultural city in America
4. Active — 100+ parks, 65+ miles trails, 270+ sunny days
5. Connected — 2nd largest city in Sacramento region
